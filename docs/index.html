Features

Small space.
Big difference.

Aeri lives in the notch and stays out of your way until you need it. Hover, glance, done.

Now Playing

Your music, wherever it plays.

Artwork, progress and controls for Apple Music, Spotify and web players in Safari, Chrome, Brave, Edge and Arc.

♫ MusicSpotifyYouTubeSpotify WebSoundCloud

Shelf

A pocket for your files.

Drop files on the notch to hold them. Drag them out wherever you need them, original names intact.

Clipboard

Everything you copied, at a glance.

Hex codes become swatches, screenshots become thumbnails, links show their site. Click to copy again.

#8B7BFF #30D158 🔗 github.com

Weather

Today, in one glance.

Conditions and today's high and low. No API key, no account, no setup beyond your city.

Details

Made to feel native.

Fluid spring animations, click-through around the notch so your menu bar keeps working, and launch at login.

Privacy

Yours stays yours.

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Your clipboard and files never leave your Mac, and password-manager copies are skipped entirely.

Install

Up and running
in a minute.

1

Download

Get the latest Aeri.zip from GitHub Releases, unzip it and move Aeri to Applications.

2

Allow it once

Aeri isn't notarized by Apple. On first launch, open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

3

Hover the notch

That's it. Turn on Launch at Login from the gear menu to keep Aeri around.
